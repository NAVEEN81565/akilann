# Phase 4: Finance and Banking

## Three-Way Matching
Three-Way Matching is a process used to ensure that the amounts on an order, invoice, and receiving report are consistent before payment is made. The importance of this process lies in preventing discrepancies and ensuring that the payments made are accurate and justifiable.

### Steps involved in Three-Way Matching:
1. **Review the Purchase Order (PO):** Verify the details outlined in the purchase order, including the item, quantity, and price.
2. **Examine the Receiving Report:** Check the receiving report to confirm that the goods received match the specifications outlined in the purchase order.
3. **Inspect the Invoice:** Ensure that the invoice provided by the vendor reflects the correct amounts based on the purchase order and the receiving report.
4. **Confirm Consistency:** Ensure that the amounts match across the three documents before processing payment.

## Accounting Entries
### Types of Entries:
- **Asset Entries:** These record the acquisition of assets, such as equipment or inventory.
- **Liability Entries:** These represent debts owed by the business, including accounts payable.
- **Equity Entries:** These describe owners' claims on assets after liabilities have been settled.

### Example Entries:
- Debit: Inventory Account
- Credit: Accounts Payable Account

## Tax Compliance
### Requirements for Compliance:
- Ensure accurate reporting of all revenue and expenses.
- Maintain proper documentation for all transactions.
- Familiarize with local, state, and federal tax regulations.

### Filing Deadlines:
- **Quarterly Payments:** Due dates for estimated tax payments.
- **Annual Returns:** Specific dates for corporate tax returns and personal tax filings.
  
## Payment Processing
### Steps in the Payment Process:
1. **Invoice Receipt and Review:** Verify the accuracy of incoming invoices and other documents.
2. **Approval Process:** Ensure that the invoices get the necessary approvals before payment.
3. **Payment Execution:** Process the payment through appropriate channels (checks, bank transfers).

### Tools and Platforms Used:
- **Accounting Software:** QuickBooks, FreshBooks, etc.
- **Payment Platforms:** PayPal, Stripe, etc.
