# Customer Workflow Documentation

## 1. Registration
   - Customers can visit the website and click on the 'Sign Up' button.
   - They will need to provide their personal details such as name, email, and password.
   - An activation link will be sent to their email for verification.
   - Once verified, they can log in to their accounts.

## 2. Order Creation
   - After logging in, customers can browse products and add them to their cart.
   - They can view their cart and proceed to checkout when ready.
   - During checkout, customers need to provide shipping and payment information.
   - Orders will be confirmed once payment is processed successfully.

## 3. Credit Validation
   - Post-order creation, the system validates the customer's credit information.
   - If the credit is valid, the order is processed; if not, the customer is notified to update their payment information.

## 4. ASN (Advance Shipping Notice)
   - After the order is processed, an ASN is generated to inform the customer of shipping details.
   - This includes tracking numbers, shipment dates, and carrier information.

## 5. Returns Management
   - Customers can initiate returns through their account within a specified time frame.
   - They must provide reasons for the return and any relevant documentation.
   - The system will generate a return authorization number and instructions for shipping the product back.
   - Once the item is received, refunds or exchanges will be processed accordingly.

This document serves as a comprehensive guide for understanding the customer workflow phases within the application.
